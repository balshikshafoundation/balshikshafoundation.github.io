Welcome to Bāl Shiksha Foundation

Every child deserves the chance to learn, grow, and step into a future filled with opportunity. We see children not as isolated individuals, but as part of families, communities, and schools that shape their everyday lives. A child’s wellbeing depends on the strength of these relationships.

Our work places children at the centre, while engaging the people and systems around them, because families, communities, and schools create conditions where learning can truly take root.

Learn more

Our mission

Millions of children in India face barriers such as poverty, migration, disability, or lack of support that prevent them from accessing education.

We at, Bāl Shiksha Foundation, believe that timely, compassionate support can change the course of a child’s life.

What we do

For many families, the cost of education, even in government or low-fee schools can be overwhelming. We help bridge these gaps so children can remain enrolled and continue learning. Depending on individual need, support may include:

  • School infrastructure
  • Uniforms, shoes, books, and stationery
  • Tuitions, transport, and enrolments
  • Financial assistance, scholarships and career guidance
  • Essential educational requirements

100% of public donations support children and educational programmes, while administrative and operational costs of the foundation are covered separately by partners.
